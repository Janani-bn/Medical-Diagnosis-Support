EXAMPLE PROMPTS — Medical Diagnosis Support & Referral Router
=============================================================

--- ROUTINE CASES (all 4 agents run: intake → triage → diagnosis → referral) ---

1. Common Cold / Flu
   "I have a runny nose, mild headache, and sore throat for the past 2 days. I am 25 years old with no prior conditions."

2. Chickenpox (with enough detail)
   "I have itchy blisters all over my body and face, including my scalp. I have a mild fever and feel tired. I am 8 years old and have not been vaccinated for chickenpox."

3. Migraine
   "I have been having severe headaches on one side of my head for the past 3 days. Light and sound bother me a lot and I feel nauseous. I am 32 years old."

4. Urinary Tract Infection
   "I feel a burning sensation when I urinate and I need to go very frequently. My lower abdomen hurts. I am a 27-year-old woman with no prior conditions."

5. Acid Reflux / GERD
   "I have a burning feeling in my chest after eating, especially at night. It feels like food is coming back up my throat. I am 40 years old and slightly overweight."

6. Skin Rash / Allergy
   "I have red itchy patches on my arms and neck that appeared after I used a new soap. There is no pus but it is very uncomfortable. I am 22 years old."

7. Back Pain
   "I have had lower back pain for a week after lifting heavy boxes. It hurts more when I sit for long periods. I am 35 years old with no prior back problems."

8. Diabetes Symptoms
   "I have been feeling very thirsty and urinating a lot. I feel tired all the time and my vision has been slightly blurry. I am 45 years old and my father had diabetes."

9. Anxiety / Panic Symptoms
   "I have been experiencing a racing heart, sweating, and shortness of breath in stressful situations. It has been happening for 2 months. I am 29 years old."

10. Pink Eye (Conjunctivitis)
    "My eyes are red, watery, and sticky with yellow discharge in the morning. It started in one eye and spread to the other. I am 15 years old."


--- URGENT CASES (triage returns urgent, all agents run) ---

11. Possible Appendicitis
    "I have severe pain in my lower right abdomen that started around my belly button and moved. I have a slight fever and feel nauseous. I am 19 years old."

12. High Fever with Rash
    "I have had a fever of 103°F for 2 days and red spots are appearing on my body. I feel very weak and have a headache. I am 30 years old."

13. Possible Kidney Infection
    "I have severe pain in my lower back and side, high fever, and chills. I also have painful urination. I am 33 years old."


--- EMERGENCY CASES (pipeline short-circuits — diagnosis and referral are skipped) ---

14. Heart Attack Symptoms
    "I have severe chest pain that is spreading to my left arm and jaw. I am sweating a lot and feel like I cannot breathe. Started 15 minutes ago."

15. Stroke Symptoms
    "Suddenly my face drooped on one side, my left arm is very weak, and my speech is slurred. This started about 20 minutes ago. I am 58 years old."

16. Severe Allergic Reaction
    "I ate peanuts and now my throat is swelling up, I cannot breathe properly, and I have hives all over my body. This is happening right now."

17. Difficulty Breathing
    "I cannot breathe. My chest feels very tight and I am gasping for air. This came on suddenly with no warning."

18. Loss of Consciousness History
    "I just woke up on the floor. I think I lost consciousness for a few minutes. I have a severe headache and I am confused. I am 50 years old."
